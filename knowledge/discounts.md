# Company-wide discounts and offers (sample data - replace with your own)

How discounts work: every car has its own discount, which is listed with that car. The brand-wide offers below are extra benefits from the manufacturer. They can be combined with the car discount.

Toyota brand offers: loyalty bonus of 20000 rupees for existing Toyota owners, corporate discount of 15000 rupees for employees of partner companies, and exchange bonus of up to 30000 rupees when trading in any old car.

Hyundai brand offers: exchange bonus of 25000 rupees on any old car, loyalty bonus of 15000 rupees for existing Hyundai owners, and special finance rate of 8.5 percent for salaried customers.

Tata Motors brand offers: exchange bonus of 20000 rupees, free home test drive, and 5 year roadside assistance on all models.

Maruti Suzuki brand offers: exchange bonus of up to 20000 rupees, corporate discount of 10000 rupees, and free first service on all models.

Festival season offer: any customer who books this month gets a free accessory kit worth 10000 rupees on any brand.

Discount approval: discounts higher than the ones listed need approval from the sales manager. Customers can ask to speak to a sales executive for negotiation.
